How Obsidian Works

A transparent look into our sophisticated analysis pipeline, from data to actionable insights.

💾

1. Data Sourcing

The process begins by pulling thousands of high-quality, recent quiz bowl questions from the extensive qbreader.org public database, ensuring a relevant and up-to-date foundation for analysis.

🧩

2. Phrase Extraction

Using advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP), each question is dissected to extract meaningful phrases. This includes single keywords (unigrams), two-word phrases (bigrams), three-word phrases (trigrams), and longer n-grams, capturing the core concepts within the text.

📈

3. TF-IDF Analysis & Filtering

We employ the Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) algorithm to score these phrases. This highlights terms that are frequent for your topic but rare across all other topics, identifying true buzzwords. Irrelevant or overly generic phrases are intelligently filtered out to ensure only the most strategic terms remain.
